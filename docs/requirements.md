# SkyVote launch requirements

What the first version of SkyVote must do. Phase one covers full moons only.

## Must-haves

1. **Next full moon.** A page showing the next full moon with a countdown.
2. **This year's full moons.** A list of this year's full moons with their dates.
3. **Names and stories.** Each full moon's traditional name and a short story behind it.
4. **Date source.** A link to the source for the dates, such as NASA or the US Naval Observatory.
5. **Static site.** No logins, accounts or AI chat yet.
6. **Privacy.** No cookies or data collection at launch, plus a short privacy page saying so.
7. **Original stories.** Moon name stories written in my own words, with sources credited.

## Content rules

- **Astronomy, not astrology.** All facts must be scientifically accurate.
- **Facts need sources.** No invented dates, figures or stories. Anything unverified is flagged until checked.

## Out of scope for launch

- Meteor showers
- Eclipses
- AI chat
- User accounts
- Voting on the site (voting happens on LinkedIn and Instagram)
