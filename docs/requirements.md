# SkyVote launch requirements

What the first version of SkyVote must do. Phase one covers full moons only.

## Audience

- **Site visitors:** friends and curious stargazers. Plain language, no jargon in the main text. Technical terms belong in a "learn more" section, if anywhere.
- **Recruiters on GitHub:** they care that I shipped and kept going. The repo stays tidy, the commit history clear and the README easy to skim.

## Must-haves

1. **Next full moon.** A page showing the next full moon with a countdown.
   - *Done when:* the page shows the date of the next full moon and a countdown to it, and switches to the following full moon once it passes.
2. **This year's full moons.** A list of this year's full moons with their dates.
   - *Done when:* every full moon in the current year is listed, and each date matches the cited source.
3. **Names and stories.** Each full moon's traditional name and a short story behind it.
   - *Done when:* every full moon in the list has a traditional name and a short story.
4. **Date source.** A link to the source for the dates, such as NASA or the US Naval Observatory.
   - *Done when:* the site links to the source used for the dates, and the link works.
5. **Static site.** No logins, accounts or AI chat yet.
   - *Done when:* the site is plain files with no backend, and has no login, sign-up or AI chat anywhere.
6. **Privacy.** No cookies or data collection at launch, plus a short privacy page saying so.
   - *Done when:* the browser shows no cookies set, the site loads no trackers or analytics, and a privacy page saying so is linked from the site.
7. **Original stories.** Moon name stories written in my own words, with sources credited.
   - *Done when:* every story is my own writing, not copied, and credits at least one source.

## Content rules

- **Astronomy, not astrology.** All facts must be scientifically accurate.
- **Facts need sources.** No invented dates, figures or stories. Anything unverified is flagged until checked.

## Out of scope for launch

- Meteor showers
- Eclipses
- AI chat
- User accounts
- Voting on the site (voting happens on LinkedIn and Instagram)
