# SkyVote

A community-built astronomy calendar, starting with full moons.

## Why

A passion project I'm building in public to learn what it takes to own a product end to end, shaped by what the community votes for.

## How voting works

Each month I post the same poll on LinkedIn and Instagram, add up the votes from both, and the top feature gets built next.

## Roadmap

- **Phase one: full moons**, with names, stories and a countdown.
- **After that:** the community votes on what's next. Early candidates are meteor showers, eclipses and an AI sky guide.

## Privacy

No cookies or data collection at launch.
